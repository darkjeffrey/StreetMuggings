# Changelog

## 1.0.3
- Updated for Bannerlord v1.5.7.
- The launcher no longer warns that the dependencies (Native, Harmony, ButterLib, UIExtenderEx, MCM) don't match: each one now states the version it needs.

## 1.0.2
- New "Gold carried on your person" setting (default 1,000; 0 = whole purse). Losses, pay-offs and the minimum-gold check use only what you carry.
- Every player-facing line, including the settings page, can be translated; `Module/ModuleData/Languages/std_StreetMuggings.xml` lists them all in English.
- The Mod Options page title no longer shows its text id.

## 1.0.0
- First release: Warband-style town muggings on the way to the market or tavern district, or during night walks, with the odds driven by town security, prosperity, nearby guards and gang-leader relations.
